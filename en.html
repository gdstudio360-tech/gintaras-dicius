01 / About me

Practical technical skills, reliability and attention to quality.

I am an Electrical & BMS Installer with practical experience in the United Kingdom. My experience includes control panels, AHU, MVHR and ASHP systems, sensors, actuators, and BACnet and Modbus communication cabling.

I can read electrical schematics, carry out installation and connection work, test circuits and trace faults. I am used to working to high standards of safety, quality and workmanship.
